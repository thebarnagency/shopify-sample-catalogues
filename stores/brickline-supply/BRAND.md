# Brickline Supply

A fictional streetwear store. Price band: £18–£320.

## Brand description
An independent menswear stockist that started as a single unit in a converted Midlands mill. It sells a tight edit of outdoor, workwear and contemporary labels next to its own Brickline basics. The shop's job is curation: fewer brands, chosen for how the clothes wear in, not for how loud they are.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Utilitarian and lived-in. Texture-heavy fabrics, earthy colourways and functional hardware.
- **Palette:** Washed Black, Clay, Moss, Oat, Signal Orange
- **Photography:** Front and back packshots on a pale concrete-grey background, plus close detail crops of buttons, pockets, labels and fabric weave. Occasional on-model shots outdoors in overcast light.
- **Tone of voice:** Knowledgeable shop-floor voice. Practical and specific about fabric weights, fits and where things are made, never hypey.

## Target customers
Men aged 25–45 who care about clothes but not trends: outdoor-curious city dwellers, trade and creative workers, and repeat buyers of a few trusted labels. They typically spend £80–£250 per item and shop the drops and the sale.

## Main collections
New In, Jackets, Trousers, Knitwear, T-Shirts, Shoes, Accessories, Sale

## Products
A multi-brand menswear catalogue. Each colourway is a separate product (one handle per colour), and the vendor field carries the stocked brand. Tops come in S–XXL; trousers use waist sizes (28W–38W) with some styles adding leg lengths. Footwear uses UK sizes in half steps (UK6–UK12). Materials include heavyweight jersey, ripstop, duck canvas, lambswool and recycled nylon. The sale section is wide, so compare-at prices are common.
