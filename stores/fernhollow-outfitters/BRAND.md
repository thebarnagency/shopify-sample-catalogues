# Fernhollow Outfitters

A fictional menswear store. Price band: £25–£495.

## Brand description
A heritage-minded menswear brand making hard-wearing shirts, chore coats and trousers in small seasonal runs. Fernhollow designs for the long haul, with organic and recycled cloth, a repairs service and a preloved section. Each season is told through a named capsule and a journal story about a maker or a place.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Vintage workwear meets coastal outdoors. Waxed cotton, waffle, cord and indigo.
- **Palette:** Fatigue Olive, Saffron, Indigo, Walnut, Brick Red
- **Photography:** A folded flat lay on a neutral linen background plus an on-model shot for every product. Campaign imagery is shot outdoors in coastal and moorland locations in natural light.
- **Tone of voice:** Warm, grounded and quietly confident. Seasonal and reflective in the journal, plain-spoken on product pages, and specific about fabric weight and construction.

## Target customers
Men aged 28–55 who want a small wardrobe that lasts: outdoorsy professionals and weekend walkers. They spend £90–£300 per piece and come back for new colourways of favourite fits.

## Main collections
New Arrivals, Shirts, Outerwear, Trousers, Knitwear, Accessories, Preloved, Last Call

## Products
Shirts and outerwear come in XS–XXL. Trousers use waist sizes (30–40 in steps of two) and some styles add a fit option (Slim / Straight / Relaxed). Named colourways carry the story, and limited runs come in several colourways. Fabrics are waxed ripstop, organic waffle, pincord, herringbone wool and dry-wax canvas. Preloved items are single-variant, one-off sizes.
