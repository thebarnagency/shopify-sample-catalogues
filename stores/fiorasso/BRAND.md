# Fiorasso

A fictional luxury jewellery store. Price band: £650–£48,000.

## Brand description
A family-run Florentine goldsmith, now in its third generation, making 18k gold jewellery by hand with hammered and textured finishes. Fiorasso pairs workshop heritage with a playful streak. Its best-known pieces are hammered gold charms inlaid with hand-fired enamel, each colour chosen for what it means to the wearer.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Warm, tactile gold with a hand-beaten texture. Colourful stones and kiln-fired enamel add colour.
- **Palette:** Rose Gold, Yellow Gold, Terracotta, Lapis Blue, Ivory Enamel
- **Photography:** Cut-out product shots on soft grey, close-up wrist and ear stacks on models in warm daylight, and atelier scenes with workbenches, hammers and gold shavings.
- **Tone of voice:** Warm, emotive and personal. It talks about meaning, gifting and craft, with Italian phrases used lightly. Self-expression is the theme, never logo status.

## Target customers
Affluent women and gift-buyers aged 30–65 marking milestones: engagements, birthdays, promotions. They spend £700–£15,000 and often come back to add to a stack or personalise a piece.

## Main collections
Bracelets, Necklaces, Earrings, Rings, Enamel Charms, High Jewellery, Personalise, Gifts

## Products
Solid 18k gold in yellow, rose and white, with two-tone pieces. Variants are gold colour plus size or chain length (40cm / 45cm / 70cm) or ring size (UK J–Q). Enamel pieces add an enamel colour option. Stones are white and champagne diamonds, sapphires, malachite and moonstone. High-jewellery pieces are single-variant and some are price on request (an edge case for zero-price handling).
