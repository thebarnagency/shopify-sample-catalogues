# Glimmerlow

A fictional jewellery store. Price band: £18–£395.

## Brand description
A London demi-fine jewellery brand built around the curated ear and the everyday stack. Glimmerlow makes gold-plated and recycled-silver pieces at gifting prices. It runs piercing and permanent-jewellery studios that turn a purchase into an appointment.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Modern, celestial and stackable. Huggies, charms, crystals and fine chains.
- **Palette:** Gold, Silver, Blush, Midnight, Crystal
- **Photography:** Tight model close-ups of styled ears and wrists, plus multi-angle product shots on a soft beige background. Seasonal flat lays for gift sets.
- **Tone of voice:** Upbeat, inclusive and community-led. It talks about "building your stack", and encourages self-gifting without guilt.

## Target customers
Women aged 18–40, from students to young professionals, buying for themselves and friends. Typical baskets are £45–£150, and big spikes come at Christmas and Valentine's.

## Main collections
New In, Earrings, Piercing Jewellery, Necklaces, Bracelets, Rings, Charms, Gifts

## Products
Titles follow "[Design] [Item] in [Finish]". Finish (Gold / Silver / Mixed Metal) is the main variant: 18k gold plating on recycled sterling silver, or rhodium plating. Earrings are sold as a pair, or as a single (an edge case). Piercing studs add a post length (6mm / 8mm / 10mm). Necklaces come in an adjustable 40–45cm length. Gift sets and advent calendars sit at the top of the range.
