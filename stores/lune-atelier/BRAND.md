# Lune Atelier

A fictional beauty store. Price band: £10–£85.

## Brand description
A skin-first make-up and care brand built around easy, low-effort products that make you look like yourself on a good day. Lune Atelier began with a tinted lip balm. It now sells sheer complexion, brows, lips and a small fragrance line, all designed to be layered.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Soft, fresh and tactile. Dewy skin, translucent textures and milky packaging.
- **Palette:** Milk, Rosewood, Apricot, Sage, Cocoa
- **Photography:** Bare-faced models in natural window light with visible skin texture. Macro swatches on skin across several skin tones, plus packshots on a pale tinted background.
- **Tone of voice:** Friendly, brief and a little cheeky, written as a confident best friend. Short taglines, lowercase moments, and no jargon.

## Target customers
People aged 18–35, mostly women, who want a quick routine rather than a full face. They are social-led, try new shades often, and build baskets of £30–£70 with sets.

## Main collections
Makeup, Skincare, Lips, Brows, Fragrance, Sets, Bestsellers

## Products
Shade-led variants: complexion runs to 12–16 shades, lip and cheek to 6–13 flavours or tints, and brows to 4 shades. Sizes are given in ml or g (15ml balm, 30ml tint, 50ml / 8ml fragrance). Sets and duos have compare-at prices that show the saving. Shade swatch images are a key image slot, and some new shades launch as "coming soon" (out of stock).
