# Maison Orlet

A fictional high fashion store. Price band: £160–£2,600.

## Brand description
A London house that treats fashion as one part of a wider craft practice. Its ready-to-wear and bags sit alongside ceramics, furniture and collaborations with makers. Orlet is known for slightly off-kilter proportions and a sculptural pebble-shaped bag that became its calling card.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Intellectual and playful. Exaggerated volumes, crafted surfaces and unexpected objects.
- **Palette:** Bone, Rust Suede, Ink, Moss Green, Chalk Pink
- **Photography:** Two tracks. E-commerce packshots on warm off-white with front, back and detail views. Still-life lookbook images pair garments with ceramics and found objects, lit with hard raking light.
- **Tone of voice:** Product titles are literal (item, material, colour). Editorial copy is dry, witty and culturally referential. Labels like [New] and [Exclusive] are used sparingly.

## Target customers
Fashion-literate buyers aged 25–50 who collect designers rather than follow them: creatives, architects, gallery people. They buy one statement piece a season (£500–£1,500) plus the odd ceramic as a gift.

## Main collections
New In, Womens, Mens, Bags, Shoes, Knitwear, Home & Objects, Collaborations

## Products
Ready-to-wear with colour as the main variant (2–5 colourways per style) and sizes XS–XL or IT 38–46. Bags, home pieces and objects are mostly single-variant. Materials include suede, mohair, double-faced wool, raw denim, glazed stoneware and solid oak. Copy leans on how each piece is made. Small runs mean some colourways sell out early.
