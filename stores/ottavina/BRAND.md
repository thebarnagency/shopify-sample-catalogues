# Ottavina

A fictional high fashion store. Price band: £220–£2,400.

## Brand description
A womenswear label that marries Milanese tailoring heritage with a London townhouse sensibility. Ottavina makes dresses and tailoring in rich Italian fabrics, cut for women who dress for dinners, openings and weddings rather than for the street. A made-to-order service runs from its Marylebone salon.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Polished, feminine and quietly opulent. Structured silhouettes in silk, velvet and cady.
- **Palette:** Taupe, Burgundy, Black, Ivory, Bottle Green
- **Photography:** Editorial lookbook images set in townhouse interiors and galleries, with art-inspired framing. On-model packshots against warm stone walls, with detail crops of drape, seams and fabric sheen.
- **Tone of voice:** Understated and brand-led. Short uppercase banners, minimal calls to action, and gentle references to Italian craft and art history.

## Target customers
Women aged 30–60 with established style and a full diary of events: professionals, hosts and wedding guests. They buy two or three pieces a season at £600–£1,500 and value fit and alterations.

## Main collections
New In, Dresses, Tailoring, Knitwear, Skirts, Coats & Jackets, Bags & Jewellery

## Products
Titles follow "[Name] [Garment] in [colour] [fabric]", and each name is an invented Italian first name or place. Woven pieces use IT 38–46 and knitwear uses XS–XL. Most styles come in one or two colours, each as its own handle. Fabrics are silk mikado, silk velvet, sable cady, merino and lurex. The made-to-measure pieces are price-on-request edge cases.
