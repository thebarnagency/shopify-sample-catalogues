# Popwire

A fictional fast fashion store. Price band: £4–£65.

## Brand description
An online-only womenswear label that drops new styles every week, chasing what is trending on social feeds that month. Popwire is about volume, newness and price. Its catalogue is wide, shallow and constantly turning over, with heavy promotions.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Trend-led and Y2K-adjacent. Going-out tops, co-ords, cargo, denim and lots of colour.
- **Palette:** Hot Pink, Cobalt, Lime, Black, Chrome Silver
- **Photography:** High-volume on-model studio shots on a flat light-grey background: front, back, side and detail. User-generated "as styled" images and short video stills supplement them.
- **Tone of voice:** Fast, hype-driven and emoji-friendly. "NEW IN", "SELLING FAST" and "back by popular demand". Trend names and short punchy titles.

## Target customers
Women aged 16–28 shopping for nights out, holidays and outfit posts. They are highly price-sensitive and promo-driven, buy several items per order (£35–£80), and return often.

## Main collections
New In, Dresses, Tops, Co-ords, Jeans & Trousers, Going Out, Petite & Tall, Sale

## Products
A wide catalogue with many colourways per style as variants (3–6 colours) and UK sizes 4–20, with Petite and Tall versions as separate handles. Materials are polyester, viscose, stretch denim and slinky jersey. There are lots of compare-at prices, low-stock flags and sold-out sizes. Titles are short and trend-coded. Co-ord pieces are sold separately and linked through a metafield.
