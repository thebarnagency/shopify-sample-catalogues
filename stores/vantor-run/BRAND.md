# Vantor Run

A fictional sports shoes store. Price band: £12–£220.

## Brand description
A performance running brand making road and trail shoes for runners who log real miles. Vantor builds each shoe around a named foam compound and publishes stack height, drop and weight up front. It sits between the big sportswear giants and boutique trail labels.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Technical and energetic. Bright accent colours on clean uppers, with visible foam and lugs.
- **Palette:** Volt Yellow, Electric Blue, Black, Fog White, Ember Red
- **Photography:** Lateral, medial, top-down and sole views on white, plus mid-stride action on road and trail with motion blur and early-morning light.
- **Tone of voice:** Spec-led and motivating. Numbers first (grams, mm, drop), then feel. It is encouraging without being cheesy.

## Target customers
Runners aged 20–55, from first-marathon trainers to seasoned trail runners. They compare spec sheets, buy two or three pairs a year (£110–£190), and replace them every 500–800km.

## Main collections
Road Running, Trail Running, Racing, Mens, Womens, Apparel, Accessories, Outlet

## Products
Shoes have Size (UK 3–13 in half sizes, with mens and womens lasts as separate handles) and Colourway variants, plus Width (Standard / Wide) on core trainers, which makes for big variant matrices. Spec metafields are central: weight (g), stack height (mm), drop (mm), surface and cushioning level. Previous-season colourways move to Outlet with compare-at prices. Socks and accessories are low-price items with size-band variants.
