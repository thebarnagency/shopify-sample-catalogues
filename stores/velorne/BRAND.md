# Velorne

A fictional fashion footwear store. Price band: £295–£995.

## Brand description
A womenswear footwear and handbag label known for sculptural mid-heels, handmade in the Marche region of Italy. Velorne designs architectural shapes that are still comfortable enough to wear all day, and treats each shoe as a small object.

## Brand style
- **Aesthetic:** Sculptural and minimal. Curved heels, pointed toes and sharp geometric cut-outs.
- **Palette:** Dark Chocolate, Cream, Black, Burgundy, Taupe
- **Photography:** Standardised side, toe, back and sole views on a clean warm-white background. Campaigns are shot like cinematic film stills, with shoes as objects in architecture.
- **Tone of voice:** Measured and design-led. It talks about form, materials and longevity, never trends or urgency.

## Target customers
Style-conscious women aged 28–55 working in creative and professional fields, who want one statement shoe a season. They spend £350–£850, and bridal is a strong secondary market.

## Main collections
New In, Heels, Mules, Flats, Boots, Sneakers, Bridal, Handbags

## Products
Each shoe style is named, with the heel height in mm in the title (e.g. "Arcus 85"), and each colour or leather is its own handle. Sizes run EU 35–41 with half sizes on core styles (35.5, 36.5 and so on), which makes for long size lists. Materials are calf leather, suede, patent, satin and raffia. Handbags are single-variant. Some sizes are on waitlist (sold-out variants).
